# Vera View privacy policy

_Last updated: October 9, 2026_

Vera View is a read-only viewer for drawing files. It works entirely on your device.

- **No data collection.** Vera View does not collect, store, or share any personal
  information, usage data, analytics, or crash reports.
- **No network access.** Vera View does not connect to the internet or to any server.
- **Your files stay on your device.** Vera View reads only the files you choose to open, and
  only to display them. It never modifies, copies, or uploads them.
- **No accounts or tracking.** There is no sign-in, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Questions: contact the publisher, Jeff Remec, through the support details on Vera View's
Microsoft Store page.
